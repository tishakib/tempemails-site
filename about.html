About tempemails.site

Our Mission for Digital Privacy

Shielding global internet citizens from unwanted spam, persistent tracking cookies, and data breaches since 2026.

Why tempemails.site Exists

In the modern era of the internet, privacy is under constant attack. Nearly every online tool, blog, Wi-Fi hotspot, and web service forces users to disclose their personal email address before granting basic access.

Once submitted, your personal inbox is frequently added to aggressive marketing mailing lists, sold to third-party ad brokers, and exposed during unintended data breaches. tempemails.site was created to solve this widespread issue permanently.

Our Core Architectural Principles

🔒 Zero Logging Policy

We never log personal identities or track IP addresses to create behavioral profiles.

⚡ Instant OTP Parsing

High-speed edge filters extract verification codes in one click, eliminating tedious message hunting.

🛡️ Inbound-Only Security

We operate strictly as an inbound receiving platform, making it impossible for spam abuse to originate from our servers.

🌐 100% Free Forever

No subscriptions, no hidden credit card traps, and no mandatory passwords required.
