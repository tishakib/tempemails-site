About tempemails.site

Our Mission for Digital Privacy

Founded with a simple vision, tempemails.site provides an impenetrable barrier between everyday internet users and malicious spam marketers, data scrapers, and phishing syndicates.

Every time you enter your real personal email on an untrusted website to download an eBook, activate a trial, or register on an online community, you risk having that address sold, scraped, or compromised in a future corporate data leak.

We created tempemails.site to make privacy effortless. With zero signups, zero phone numbers, and up to 5 concurrent disposable inboxes, you can test apps, verify accounts, and discard the address instantly.
