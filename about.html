Why tempemails.site Exists

In the modern era of the internet, privacy is under constant attack. Nearly every online tool, blog, Wi-Fi hotspot, and web service forces users to disclose their personal email address before granting basic access.

Once submitted, your personal inbox is frequently added to aggressive marketing mailing lists, sold to third-party ad brokers, and exposed during unintended data breaches. tempemails.site was created to solve this widespread issue permanently.
