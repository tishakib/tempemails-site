Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our temporary email features, limits, and privacy protection.

What is a temporary disposable email?
A temporary disposable email (also known as 10-minute mail, burner email, or fake email) is a short-lived email address that allows you to receive incoming mail without requiring personal registration or passwords. Once you are done with the account verification, the email expires automatically.
How many inboxes can I create at once?
On tempemails.site, you can create and manage up to 5 concurrent inboxes simultaneously. You can easily switch between them using the top tabs. If you reach the 5-inbox limit, you can delete any existing inbox to free up a slot for a new one.
How long do received emails stay in the inbox?
Received emails are available during your active browsing session. For privacy and storage optimization, messages are automatically expunged after expiration. We strongly advise copying your OTP codes or verification links immediately.
Can I send emails using tempemails.site?
No. In order to protect our servers and global reputation from being blacklisted by anti-spam filters, our platform operates strictly as an inbound-only receiving relay. Outbound email sending is disabled.
Is tempemails.site safe for banking or password resets?
No! Never use disposable email for critical accounts. Since temporary addresses are public and disposable, you will not be able to recover your account if you forget your password in the future. Use temp mail strictly for non-critical signups, trial services, and spam prevention.
