Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At tempemails.site, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by tempemails.site and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We operate under a strict Zero-Logging Architecture:

  • We do NOT require real names, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do NOT track personal identities or link disposable email addresses to your real identity.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal communications.

2. Inboxes and Message Retention Policy

Temporary mailboxes generated on tempemails.site are ephemeral. Incoming emails are retained on our secured edge nodes for up to 7 days to give you ample time to access your verification codes or activation links. Once expired, messages are permanently purged from all memory and disk storage and cannot be restored.

3. Cookies and Browser Fingerprinting

tempemails.site utilizes standard local storage and client-side device hashes solely for session synchronization—ensuring that you can switch between active inbox slots and prevent robotic service abuse. We do not use persistent cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to tempemails.site and other websites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

Our third-party advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense (Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have the right to request deletion of any temporary session data at any time by clicking "Delete" on any active inbox tab.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@tempemails.site.
