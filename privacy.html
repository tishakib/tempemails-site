Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At tempemails.site, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by tempemails.site and how we utilize it.

1. What Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional mail services, tempemails.site does not require personal registration, passwords, phone numbers, or credit card information. We do not maintain user accounts or personal profiles.

2. Inbound Email Data Retention

Incoming messages sent to temporary addresses are held temporarily in volatile memory/cache solely to enable users to view their verification codes and messages. Messages are scheduled for automatic permanent deletion after expiration. Once deleted, emails cannot be recovered by anyone, including our system administrators.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, tempemails.site uses standard cookies and local storage (such as HTML5 LocalStorage) solely to remember your active 5-inbox slots on your current browser so you don't lose your addresses when refreshing the page.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to tempemails.site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on tempemails.site, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of personal information. Because tempemails.site collects zero identifying personal records, no identifying user dossiers exist on our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@tempemails.site.
