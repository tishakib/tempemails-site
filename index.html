Frequently Asked Questions

What is tempemails.site?

tempemails.site is a free disposable email service that allows you to receive emails at a temporary address without registration. It helps keep your personal email safe from spam, advertising mailings, and malware.

How many emails can I create?

You can manage up to 5 concurrent active inboxes at the same time. If you need a new one, you can delete an existing one or click "Change" to regenerate it.

How long do my messages remain valid?

Messages are preserved for your active session and automatically purged from our servers to protect your privacy. Do not use temporary email for long-term critical accounts like banking.

Can I send emails from tempemails.site?

No, tempemails.site is strictly an inbound-only receiving service to eliminate any possibility of spam abuse or outbound harassment.

Disclaimer & Acceptable Use: tempemails.site is designed for testing, privacy protection, and one-off registrations. Never use temporary disposable emails for banking, cryptocurrency wallets, tax filings, or sensitive legal communications.
