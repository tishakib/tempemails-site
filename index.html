Frequently Asked Questions

What is tempemails.site?

tempemails.site is a free disposable email service that allows you to receive emails at a temporary address without registration. It helps keep your personal email safe from spam, advertising mailings, and tracking.

How many temporary inboxes can I create and manage?

You can manage up to 5 concurrent active inboxes simultaneously. You can switch between active slots with 1 click, regenerate an address using the “Change” button (up to 3 times per inbox), or click “Delete” to free up slot capacity.

How long do my temporary messages remain valid?

Emails and inboxes are retained for up to 7 days before being automatically purged from our servers, or until you choose to delete them. This gives you ample time to complete email verification without losing activation codes unexpectedly.

Do my temporary inboxes survive a browser refresh?

Yes. Your active inbox slots and messages are synchronized with your browser's local storage. You can refresh the page or close your browser without losing your active addresses during their retention window.

Can I send emails from tempemails.site?

No. tempemails.site is strictly an inbound-only receiving service to eliminate any possibility of spam abuse or outbound harassment.

What should I do if a website rejects my temporary email?

Some websites filter known disposable email domains to enforce long-term lead capture. You can try regenerating a fresh address using the “Change” button, or consult our dedicated guide on Why Is My Temporary Email Rejected? to diagnose form validation versus delivery issues.
